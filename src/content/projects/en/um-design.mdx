## The context

### A heavy site with little control over the result

Uirá's site was built in Mobirise, a visual website builder. The result was heavy, hard to update, and left little control over the code, the design, and the performance. I rebuilt the site as a single static page in Astro, with a few parts in React.

Content lives in structured YAML, and Zod validates that content at build time. As a result, the site needs no CMS, no database, and no backend. It loads fast and sends visitors straight to WhatsApp. I can update the content with a simple commit. It also collects no personal data.
