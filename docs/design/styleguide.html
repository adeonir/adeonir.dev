Spacing

4px base grid. Intra-component in the 2–6 band; section separation jumps to 16–24.

Radius

Gently rounded and consistent — considered software, not playful.

Border Width

Hairline does the work; emphasis at 2px for the eyebrow outline.

Elevation

Depth is tone-first; shadows stay a whisper — depth reads through tone and hairline, not lift.

Motion & Easing

Loops automatically. Ease-out is the default for entrances and hovers.
