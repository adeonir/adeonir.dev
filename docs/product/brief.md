# Brief: adeonir.dev Personal Portfolio

## What

adeonir.dev is a personal portfolio for Adeonir Kohl, a frontend developer with
6+ years building web interfaces, primarily in React and TypeScript. Today he
has no web presence that represents him, which leaves three audiences — clients,
recruiters, and peers — without a single place to evaluate his work and decide
whether to engage. The site solves this by giving each of them a clear, credible
home: who he is, the work he has shipped, and a way to reach him.

## Why

Most developer portfolios read the same and prove little. Adeonir's edge is a
prior career in graphic design: a designer's eye applied to engineering —
design + code — so he builds interfaces that are both well-crafted and fast,
aesthetics and performance, not one at the expense of the other. He leans on AI assistants and
agentic workflows for the heavy lifting, always under human supervision, which
lets him move quickly without giving up craft or judgment. Over the past year he
has been growing from frontend into product engineering, owning more of the
product than the UI alone. The site itself is the proof: its design and
performance demonstrate the claim instead of asserting it.

## Who

The primary visitor is a prospective client confirming that someone can handle
both look and speed before starting a conversation. Recruiters come next, judging
fit quickly and deciding whether to reach out; peers come to gauge craft and
taste. Across all three the work leads and contact follows. They choose Adeonir
over a generic portfolio because the work — and the site presenting it — show the
design-plus-performance combination directly.

## Scope

- A landing surface that establishes identity, proof, and positioning
- A curated selection of work that leads into the full index
- A full work index and a per-project detail surface
- A contact path: a form plus at least one direct channel
- Persistent navigation across every surface

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target | How to Measure |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Site authentically represents Adeonir | Owner agrees it represents him well | Owner self-assessment (qualitative) |

Usage statistics (work views, contact submissions) are collected via lightweight,
privacy-respecting analytics for insight only — not a success metric, no targets.

## Risks

- The primary goal is qualitative; analytics observe usage but set no targets, so there is no pass/fail signal that the site lands with its audiences.
- The "designer's eye" differentiator is a common claim; it must be shown through the work and the site, not stated.
- Positioning leads on craft (shorthand: "design + code"); 6 years anchors credibility, but the design background and the work must prove the claim, not just assert it.
