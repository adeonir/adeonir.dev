# Product: adeonir.dev Personal Portfolio

## Register

`brand`. The site is the product — a personal portfolio, not a task tool — and its own design and performance are the proof of the claim it makes. There is no exception surface to plan for.

## Users

The primary relationship is expert-to-expert: a prospective client confirms, without a sales pitch, that someone can handle both look and speed before starting a conversation. Recruiters come next, judging fit quickly and deciding whether to reach out; peers come to gauge craft and taste as one professional sizing up another. Across all three the work leads and contact follows — the site earns attention on its own terms rather than asking for it.

## Product Purpose

adeonir.dev is Adeonir Kohl's personal portfolio, and its own construction is the argument: a designer's eye applied to engineering, so the interfaces it presents — and the site presenting them — are both well-crafted and fast, aesthetics and performance together, never one at the expense of the other. It exists to give clients, recruiters, and peers one credible place to evaluate that claim directly instead of taking it on faith.

## Brand Personality

Craft-driven, personal, casual. Craft-driven because every surface treats design and code as one discipline, not a hand-off between them. Personal because the site speaks as Adeonir, not as an agency or a brand voice — first person, direct, no committee-speak. Casual because confidence here reads as ease, not formality — the site never oversells itself to compensate for a weak claim.

## Anti-references

Rejects the generic template portfolio: the recognizable stock layout, stock copy, and stock component choices that signal a theme rather than a person. Anything that reads as "picked from a gallery" undermines the core claim that design judgment is the differentiator.

## Design Principles

- The site demonstrates the claim instead of asserting it — its own design and performance are evidence, not a testimonial.
- Aesthetics and performance are one requirement, never traded against each other.
- Work leads, contact follows — every surface optimizes for showing the work first.
